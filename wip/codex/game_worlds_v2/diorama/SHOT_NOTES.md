- **Time:** 48.3 s, during “Make me real this time.” This still shows **MAKE ME REAL** because those words begin at 47.42, 47.77, and 48.09 s; “this” and “time” arrive later at 48.86 and 49.16 s.
- **Game identity:** oblique HD-2D code-world micro-diorama, not the existing straight corridor. Enormous `{ }` braces are retaining walls; `[` railings, `===` and `+++` extrusions, and `^` spikes are load-bearing level geometry. The approved Q1, bold, 17×27 glyph girl stands on an indented code-line platform. Only her symbol heart is orange.
- **Depth planes:** blurred syntax stacks in the near frame edge; sharp central indented line and support characters; smaller, increasingly defocused expressions and the bracket cursor gate; matte black void. Angular cast shadows separate each plane. Keep the sprite flat while the camera changes angle only a little.
- **Motion:** cut in on a beat to a wide view; bring **MAKE**, **ME**, **REAL** on their vocal onsets. Near the “real” onset, a line of `===` tokens extrudes toward the far `[]` cursor gate. At “this time,” the final token fails to evaluate and drops out; the heart stays visible and the camera nudges closer to her. Each syntax line moves at a different parallax rate. An enormous `{` can wipe across the frame at the cut.
- **Build:** built-in imagegen created the original empty depth/composition plate. `compose_diorama.py` then uses its brightness strictly as a tone map and **rebuilds the entire environment from printed `. : = + x % @ #` glyphs**, with exact monospaced characters at 9×14 px cells. It places the project's `final_sheet.py` girl with its bold stroke and knockout. No reference game's characters or assets were used. For a moving production scene, rebuild the code walls, bridge and floor as separate animated glyph planes rather than scaling this still.
